Cleaning and missing values: listings.csv
================================================================================
Rows: 91,954 -> 59,905
Columns: 90 -> 78
Cells with missing values: 1,933,263 -> 215,401

Steps taken
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Dropped 14 completely empty columns, because they hold no information: neighborhood_overview, host_since, host_response_time, host_response_rate, host_acceptance_rate, host_thumbnail_url, host_neighbourhood, host_total_listings_count, host_verifications, neighbourhood, neighbourhood_group_cleansed, calendar_updated, license, instant_bookable. bathrooms is kept, because it is rebuilt from bathrooms_text.
2. Removed 0 duplicate listings (same id).
3. Converted price from text such as '$1,180.00' to a number by removing the '$' and the thousands commas.
4. Dropped 32,049 rows without a price. Price is the main variable for analysis, so filling it in would invent the values we want to study. Most of these listings have no available days in the next year, so they cannot be booked anyway.
5. Flagged 600 listings with a price above the 99th percentile (1,272.16) in price_above_p99. They are kept, because they may be genuine luxury listings.
6. Filled 59,813 missing bathrooms from the number in bathrooms_text (e.g. '1.5 baths' -> 1.5).
7. Filled 92 missing bathrooms with the median of listings that accommodate the same number of guests.
8. Filled 24 missing bedrooms with the median of listings that accommodate the same number of guests.
9. Filled 1,502 missing beds with the median of listings that accommodate the same number of guests.
10. Filled 12,258 missing reviews_per_month with 0, only for listings with no reviews.
11. Added a has_reviews column (12,258 listings have no reviews). Their review scores are left missing, because filling them in would give unreviewed listings a typical score they never got.
12. Filled 3 missing has_availability from availability_365: 't' when the listing has any available days in the next year.
13. Converted the 't'/'f' columns to True/False: host_is_superhost, host_has_profile_pic, host_identity_verified, has_availability.
14. Stored counts such as bedrooms and beds as whole numbers (1, not 1.0).
